Guardia de Mieux
a.k.a.
The Jaquio
NG 2
  Guardia de Mieux ("Guardian of Men"), a.k.a. the Jaquio, took over the Amazon ruins of the Temple of the Demon at the behest of his evil masters.  However, the promise of power proved too great for this powerful warlock and he decided to rule the world himself.  His encounter with Ryu Hayabusa, the Ninja Dragon, would prove otherwise.
   Wiser, he returned again to bring the world into darkness for his evil master, the Demon, rather than for himself.  He failed again.
Ninja Gaiden и связанные изображения — собственность Tecmo. Тексты и оформление исходных страниц принадлежат Guardian. Это адаптированная раскладка архива; недостающие анимации оригинала заменены статичными иконками.
