Welcome and thanks for coming!
  Welcome to the lair of possibility! 
   A reminder, this is not an official site, nor is Ninja Gaiden IV: Darkness Reborn (or Final Confrontation, it's original title) a game made by any company that I know of.  This site is dedicated to an idea I've had for the last ten years.  Considering that there will be no more money to be made in the 8- to 16-bit realms, I'm free to share these concepts and animations (purely my work, mind you) and hope that you enjoy them.  The storyline is a work in progress, and I welcome any feedback, but it's also part of how everything is as you'll see it in this site.
   Perhaps someday, I'll endeavor to make this site into the be-all, end-all of Ninja Gaiden, but for now, it's just an anim paradise.  If you have any requests, please let me know.  The anim above, for instance, is something I thought I'd do to show my appreciation and best wishes for my former brethren, the U.S. Army.
   I will update as often as I can.  Besides being a husband and a father, I am also working with the great Mottzilla to create Ninja Gaiden IV as an actual game!  That's right, campers ... my dream's coming true!  Courtesy of the Gaming Universe (link available in Links page), Mottzilla and I are creating Ninja Gaiden IV: Darkness Reborn.  Along with a few others who will later be credited, this project is under way, going strong, and will finally give Ninja Gaiden the respect it deserves with this ultimate tribute!
   Thanks again for coming by!
