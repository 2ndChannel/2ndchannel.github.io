  The Demon, the master of all evil (in this game), was the nastiest creature to plague the world.  In ancient times, this mighty creature faced the noble Dragon, but was eventually defeated and banished to another dimension.  This powerful abhorration brought forth minions to free it from the bounds of its prison.
   In the first adventure, the joining of the Demon Statues within the Temple of the Demon gave the imprisoned Demon its power back.  Yet it wasn't enough to destroy our hero, the Ninja Dragon.
   In the second chapter, it was released again from the bounds of Hell by the Sword of Chaos.  Once again, Ryu was able to destroy it.
   But was that truly the last of the Demon?
