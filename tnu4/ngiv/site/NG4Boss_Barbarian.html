The Barbarian
  Being the very first boss to face down the Ninja Dragon, and obviously the weakest, this member of the Malice Four was revived by the Jaquio with more stength, better skills and sharper reflexes.  Now donning an axe, the Barbarian fully intends to split his enemies in two...even if it means taking the battle to the air.  Unfortunately for him, he may be too strong for his own good.
  This is all he does, but not all he's capable.  He's learned a thing or two from trying the direct approach with Ryu.  If you get too close at the wrong time, he'll be happy to blitz you BEFORE he chops you in two with his devastating Log Cutter attack!
