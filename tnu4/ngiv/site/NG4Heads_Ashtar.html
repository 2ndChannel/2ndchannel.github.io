Lord Ashtar
Being the toughest hombre in the game, Ashtar's naturally going to have (a) a heavy repertoire, and (b) the speed and intensity to use them well.
Be warned: His swordsmanship is unrivaled.  Only a true ninja  master can defeat him this time around.  He's both patient AND desperate!

Chief among the resurrected Demon Clan is Ashtar.  Given power above all others, almost like a partner, the "one who crosses the sky in flames" plays a critical part in the plot against the Ninja Dragon, his new bride, and their meddlesome partner in the most perfect ways ... but what could these ways possibly be?
As ineffective as his magical escape tactics were in the past, he has refined them, but decided to take the Ninja Dragon sword-to-sword, should it come to that.  In the millenia of Ashtar's existence, no man has ever matched his swordsmanship.  Not that his magics will go unused, but his blade will be far more formidable a method.  No one else ever survived it.
