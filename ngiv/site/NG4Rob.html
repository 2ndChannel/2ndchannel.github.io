Welcome back to the battle, Jungle Rat Rob!!!
  A career soldier, Robert T.S. was seasoned for specialized combat.  Unfortunately for him AND the government that sent him in, the Demon Clan was more than specialized, and as a result, Robert nearly met his fate.
   Amidst the battle, Robert was seriously wounded, but he volunteered to keep a horde of henchmen off Ryu's back long enough to succeed in destroying the Sword of Chaos, as well as the Jaquio and the Demon for the second time.  Fortunately, Robert found an opening and escaped, luckily able to get medical attention from the Special Forces team that extracted him.
  After that fateful night, Robert was further assigned to follow up on his case, ordered to protect Ryu from all possible attacks from the rogue agent, A. Foster.  But when Ryu went after Foster after Irene's supposed elimination, Robert stealthly followed him in.  Stalking the Castle Rock Fortress just before Ryu could reach it, Robert was attacked by sentries and captured.  Foster then decided to use the opportunity to use a familiar face against Ryu, and blasted Robert with Life Energy.  Unaware that Robert had successfully freed himself from his restraints, Foster told him of his new talents.  Determined to follow orders instead of Foster, he escaped, aborting his mission.
Upon his return to base, Robert took a leave of absense and despite his orders, he revealed his survival and tale to the newly-wedded Ryu and Irene Hayabusa.  From there, they proceeded to rebuild what the Demon Clan had torn down...the Dragon Clan.
   Educated in the Arts of the Dragon Clan, Robert took to the ways and legends of the Tiger Warrior, which suited him better.  He then proceeded to educate himself on the ways of the Tiger.  Using his Life Energy gifts, he was able to successfully master the Art of the Tiger Warrior, the transformation of body and spirit into a powerful being of courage, strength, and agility...far beyond his own.
  When word of Irene's abduction hit him, he incorporated all of his military might to aid him and Ryu in their jaunts across the world.
