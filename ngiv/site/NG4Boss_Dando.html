Dando, the Cursed
  This concoction of lost Viking warrior souls was bested once before for his lack of speed, skill, and ... pretty much everything.  Returning to the battle, the souls within him speak in unison, preaching strength, tactics, and most of all ... patience.  Don't be fooled, though...he's still a hard-charger.
  His repertoire isn't too vast or involved, but his strength will back it up well enough to be worth sweating over.
